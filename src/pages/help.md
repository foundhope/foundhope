A quick guide for Nick and Johan. Every job is a few taps, on a phone or a computer.

## The golden rule

1. Go to **foundhope.store/studio** and sign in.
2. Pick what you want to change from the menu on the left.
3. Make your change.
4. Press the green **Publish** button at the bottom.

The website updates by itself about **a minute** after you press Publish. If you don't see your change, wait two minutes and refresh the page.

> **Nothing is live until you press Publish.** If you close the page without publishing, your changes are saved as a draft and wait for you.

## Everyday jobs

### Close early, or open on a bank holiday

1. **Opening hours** → **Special days** → **Add item**.
2. Pick the **Date** and give it a **Name** (for example "Boxing Day").
3. Tick **Closed all day**, or put in the opening and closing times.
4. Press **Publish**.

Special days show on the website from six weeks before, and disappear the day after.

### Change the normal opening hours

1. **Opening hours** → **Normal week**.
2. Tap the row to change. Times use the 24-hour clock, for example **07:00** or **19:30**.
3. Press **Publish**.

### Put up a notice

For things like "Closed Monday for a private event".

1. **Notice banner and contact**.
2. Turn on **Show the banner** and type the **Message**. Add a **Link** if you want people to tap through.
3. Press **Publish**.

Turn it off again the same way when you're done.

### Add an event or tasting

1. **Events** → the **+** (create) button.
2. Fill in the name, date, times and what it is. Add a photo, a price and a booking link if you have them.
3. Turn on **Show on the home page** if it's a big one.
4. Press **Publish**.

Past events disappear from the website by themselves.

### Johan's wine picks

1. **Food & Drink page** → **Wine** tab.
2. Under **The bottles**, change the three wines: name, producer, a line on why it's good, and the price.
3. Press **Publish**.

To hide the picks for a while, turn off **Show "Johan's picks"**.

### Coffee menu and prices

1. **Food & Drink page** → **Coffee** tab → **Drinks menu**.
2. Tap a drink to change it, or **Add item** for a new one. Prices are in pounds, for example **3.40**.
3. Turn off **On the menu** to hide something without deleting it.
4. Press **Publish**.

## Christmas

### Mark something as sold out

1. **Christmas** → **The Christmas list** → open the section (for example "Sladesdown poultry").
2. Tap the item and turn off **Available to order**.
3. Press **Publish**.

It shows "Sold out" on the website and can't be ordered.

### Change a price

1. Same place as above. Tap the item.
2. Fixed price: change the **Price**. Sold by size: change the price on each **Size**.
3. Press **Publish**.

### Dates and deposit

**Christmas** → **Dates, deposit and truffles**. Here you can change:

- **Christmas orders are open**: switch the whole Christmas page on or off
- **Last day to order online**: the order form closes itself after this day
- **Last day to cancel for a full refund**
- **Last day to order in the shop**
- **Collection days** and **Collect by** time
- **Deposit (%)**

### Finding Christmas orders

Every order is a payment in **Stripe**.

1. Go to **dashboard.stripe.com** → **Payments**.
2. Tap a payment. Scroll down to **Metadata** to see the name, phone, collection day, what they ordered and any notes.

### Refunding a deposit

1. In Stripe, open the payment.
2. Press **Refund** and confirm.

The money goes back to their card in 5 to 10 working days. Stripe keeps its small fee.

## Suppliers

### Add a supplier

1. **Suppliers** → **+** (create).
2. Fill in the name, then press **Generate** next to **Web address**.
3. Add what they do, where they're from, a one-line description, a photo and their website.
4. Tick **Served here** and/or **Take home**.
5. Press **Publish**.

### Hide a supplier

Turn off **Show on the website**. Their page disappears, but nothing is deleted.

> **Don't change a supplier's Web address once their page is live.** Old links to the page would stop working.

## Photos

- Good light, steady hands, and fill the frame with the food.
- Every photo asks you to **Describe the photo** in a few words, for example "Sourdough loaves on the counter". It helps blind visitors and Google.
- Phone photos are fine. The website resizes them for you.

## The newsletter

People who sign up on the website appear in **Mailchimp** → **Audience**, tagged **Website**. They're only added once they tap the link in the confirmation email.

## If something goes wrong

- **Made a mistake?** Open the item, tap the **⋯** menu at the top right, choose **History**, pick an earlier version and restore it. Changes from the last few days can be undone this way.
- **Change not showing?** Check you pressed **Publish**. Wait two minutes and refresh the website.
- **Deleted something by accident, or still stuck?** Message Tom.

**Tip:** if in doubt, turn things off rather than deleting them. You can always turn them back on.
